# 50 additions (October 2026)

The owner asked for 50 useful additions, a professional opening, a check of every
screen size, past purchases shown after buying, customer location and keeping
customer data safe. Each item below is small enough to ship complete and tested.

## Customers (store)

1. Opening screen: the brand mark and product cards animate in (vector, sharp at any resolution), once per visit, skippable.
2. Installable app (PWA): manifest, icons, theme colour, "add to home screen" prompt.
3. Right after buying, the order page lists the customer's earlier purchases.
4. Orders page: filter by status and search by order number or product.
5. "أكوادي": every delivered code in one place.
6. Favourites: a heart on brands, and a favourites page (saved to the account).
7. Recently viewed brands on the home page.
8. Saved account IDs (player ID, username…) per game: filled in automatically, managed from the account page.
9. Profile: country and city (customer location), language and currency kept on the account.
10. Cart warns when a price changed since the item was added, and updates it.
11. "Tell me when the price drops" on a product.
12. "Tell me when it is back" on an out-of-stock amount.
13. Notifications centre (bell): payment confirmed, delivered, transfer rejected, price drop.
14. Notifications are created by the server on each order event.
15. Reviews (stars + comment) after delivery, shown on the brand page with the average.
16. "Problem with this order": a support request from the order page, with replies.
17. Coupon field at checkout.
18. Wallet: balance and history.
19. Search suggestions while typing.
20. Best sellers on the home page (from real sales).
21. "New" badge on products added in the last 14 days.
22. Countdown on offers that have an end date.
23. Share a brand (WhatsApp / phone share sheet).
24. Send a delivered code to a friend on WhatsApp (gift).
25. Printable receipt for an order.
26. Delivery-time estimate on each product ("usually within 10 minutes").
27. Exchange rate shown in the currency menu (1 $ = … MRU).
28. Trust numbers on the home page (orders delivered, customers) from real counts.
29. Offline page and friendlier error states.
30. Every page checked at phone, tablet and desktop widths; text kept off the edges.

## Owner (dashboard)

31. Dashboard: today's sales and profit, transfers to check, codes waiting, low stock.
32. Low code-stock alerts.
33. Margin alerts: products sold below cost or under the target margin, with a fix link.
34. Bulk price change by category (± %), with a preview before saving.
35. New order: sound and browser notification while the dashboard is open.
36. Customers by country and city.
37. Customer page: orders, spend, notes and tags (VIP, watch).
38. Internal notes on orders.
39. CSV export of orders and customers.
40. Full backup download (customers, orders, products, settings) — the free database expires.
41. Coupons: create, pause, see usage.
42. Reviews: hide or answer.
43. Support requests inbox with replies.
44. WhatsApp quick replies used on the order page.
45. Duplicate a product.
46. Activity log (who changed what).
47. Exchange-rate staleness warning on the dashboard.
48. Message all customers (in-app notification).
49. Orders filters: payment method, country, date range.
50. Yesterday vs today summary on the dashboard.
